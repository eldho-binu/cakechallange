⭐ OFFICIAL PARISH WORKBOOK

Complete 9-Worksheet Master Excel Workbook

Contains separate styled worksheets for All Orders, Individual Cake Items, Registered Members (excluding confidential hashes), Customer Registry, Complete Payment Transactions, Member Performance Summary, Location Breakdown, Cake Variety Statistics, and Audited Financial Summary.

Worksheets Included in Master Export

1. All Orders

Every confirmed order with Customer Name, Phone, Member, Date, Total, Paid, Balance, and Delivery notes.

2. Order Items

Detailed breakdown of every cake variety, selected weight, quantity ordered, and locked historical price.

3. Members

Complete youth roster with contact numbers, wards, and status. Codes and hashes strictly excluded.

4. Customer Details

Parish customer contacts, residential addresses, and landmarks for delivery logistics.

5. Payment History

Every single payment transaction with payment method, UPI reference, date, time, and verification state.

6. Member Summary

Leaderboard showing total cakes sold, gross sales, collections, and outstanding balance per member.

7. Location Summary

Ward-wise order totals, revenue generated, collections, and pending delivery balances.

8. Cake Summary

Total units sold and revenue per cake variety and weight (Plum Cake, Chocolate Cake, etc.).

9. Financial Summary

High-level audited financials: Gross Sales, Verified Collections, Unverified Payments, and Net Outstanding.
